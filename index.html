About Us

About Macron Industries

Macron Industries is a growing manufacturer of high-quality borewell submersible pumps built for Indian conditions. We focus on delivering reliable, long-lasting, and efficient pumping solutions for farmers, households, builders, and industrial applications.

Our products are designed with precision engineering, tested rigorously, and built using high-grade materials to ensure consistent performance even in tough environments. We are committed to building trust through quality, service, and strong dealer relationships.
